# The book is the only record

Visits, Observations, statistics, and Backup stay in the book. Tag reading, the shared save from the camera and the typed form, and loading or saving the book only prepare what the book already accepts. Splitting statistics or Backup out was rejected because a later change would then have two places that can disagree about a price.

**Status:** accepted

## Considered options

- Split statistics, Backup, and duplicate checks out of the book into their own modules.
- Keep one book. Chosen. The other modules do not store a price.
